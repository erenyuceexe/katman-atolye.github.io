# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

[Inferred from the request] People who have a 3D model file or want to choose a ready-made model, and need a quick way to request a quote for a 3D print.

## Product Purpose

[Inferred from the request] A lightweight 3D-print order intake site. A visitor chooses or uploads a model, selects Economy, Fast, or Quality print settings, shares contact details, and sends the request so the operator can follow up before printing.

## Positioning

[Inferred from the request] It turns a technical print request into a short, human handoff: the customer does not need to understand slicing or machine settings, and the operator receives the exact model and quality preference to start the conversation.

## Operating Context

[Inferred from the request] The site is hosted as a static GitHub Pages website. Orders are currently prepared as an email handoff; the owner confirms price, material, and timing directly with the customer before production.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Model source: upload a supported 3D model file (STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY, FBX, GLTF, GLB, DAE, or AMF) up to 100 MB, or select an available template.
- Quality choices: Economy, Fast, and Quality.
- Contact fields: name, email, phone, and optional order note.
- No payment or automatic quote is promised.
- Order requests are sent to yagizerenyuce@gmail.com through a static-form upload endpoint; the endpoint's own attachment limit may be lower than the browser-side 100 MB limit.

## Product Principles

- Make the first request feel easy and concrete.
- Collect only what the operator needs for a useful reply.
- Be explicit that final price and timing are confirmed after review.
- Keep technical language optional and approachable.
